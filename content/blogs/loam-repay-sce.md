## Introduction

When you take a loan, your EMI stays the same every month — but what changes inside that EMI is pure magic (or tragedy, if you don't understand it).

In the early years, **80–90% of your EMI goes toward interest**. Only in the later years does the principal repayment speed up.

This concept is called **Loan Amortization** — and understanding it can help you save lakhs through smart prepayments.

Let's break it down with real Indian examples.

## What is Loan Amortization?

Amortization is the process of paying off a loan over time through fixed EMIs. Each EMI has two parts:
- **Interest** on the outstanding loan
- **Principal** repayment

As the outstanding loan reduces, interest portion decreases → principal portion increases.

### Example: ₹50 Lakh Home Loan @ 8.5% for 20 Years

| Month | EMI (₹)   | Interest Paid | Principal Paid | Outstanding Loan |
|-------|-----------|----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1     | 43,391    | 35,417         | 7,974          | 49,92,026         |
| 60    | 43,391    | 29,100         | 14,291         | 43,15,000         |
| 120   | 43,391    | 20,500         | 22,891         | 32,10,000         |
| 240   | 43,391    | 3,000          | 40,391         | 0                 |

**Total Interest Paid: ₹54.14 lakh** (more than the loan amount itself!)

*Early EMIs = Mostly Interest | Later EMIs = Mostly Principal*

## The EMI Formula (You Don't Need to Memorize)

**EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n – 1]**

Where:
- P = Loan amount
- r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
- n = Number of monthly installments

## Prepayment & Part-Payment: Save Lakhs in Interest

## Latest Prepayment & Foreclosure Rules in India (2025)

| Loan Type            | Prepayment Charges (Floating Rate) | Prepayment Charges (Fixed Rate) | Foreclosure Allowed? |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Home Loan            | Zero (RBI rule)                     | Up to 2–4%                      | Yes (no charges for floating) |
| Loan Against Property | Zero (floating)                    | Up to 4%                         | Yes                   |
| Personal Loan        | 2–5% of prepaid amount             | 3–5%                             | Yes (with charges)    |
| Car Loan             | 2–4%                                | 3–5%                             | Yes                   |

**Pro Tip:** Always choose **floating rate** home loans — zero prepayment penalty!

## Types of Amortization Schedules

1. **Straight Line (Equal Principal)** → Principal fixed, EMI reduces over time (rare in India)
2. **Reducing Balance (Most Common)** → Fixed EMI, changing interest/principal split
3. **Bullet Repayment** → Interest monthly, full principal at end (used in short-term loans)

## Smart Strategies to Reduce Loan Burden in 2025

- [ ] Increase EMI by 10–20% every year (with salary hike)
- [ ] Prepay 1–2 EMIs worth every year from bonus
- [ ] Switch to lower interest rate via balance transfer (save 0.5–2%)
- [ ] Use windfalls (inheritance, bonus) to knock off principal
- [ ] For home loans: Opt for 15–20 year tenure, prepay aggressively

## Conclusion

Your EMI is not just a monthly burden — it's a powerful tool. Understand amortization, track your repayment schedule, and use prepayments wisely.

A ₹50 lakh home loan can be closed in 12–13 years instead of 20 with just ₹2–3 lakh annual prepayments — saving ₹25–30 lakh in interest.

Borrow smart. Repay smarter.
