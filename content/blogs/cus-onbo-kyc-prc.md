## Types of KYC Allowed in India (2025)

| KYC Type              | Time Taken       | Documents Needed       | Use Case                              |
|-----------------------|------------------|------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Aadhaar OTP eKYC      | 30 seconds       | Aadhaar + Mobile       | Up to ₹1 lakh/month transactions      |
| Video KYC (V-CIP)     | 3–5 minutes      | Aadhaar/PAN + Live video | Full banking, demat, unlimited limits |
| DigiLocker KYC        | 1–2 minutes      | Aadhaar-linked DigiLocker | Mutual funds, insurance, demat        |
| Offline Aadhaar XML   | 2 minutes        | Downloaded XML + OVD   | When mobile not linked                |
| CKYC (Central KYC)    | Instant (if done)| 14-digit CKYC number   | One-time KYC for all institutions     |

## Step-by-Step: How Video KYC Works in 2025

1. Enter Aadhaar → OTP sent to registered mobile  
2. Live video call starts (agent or fully automated AI)  
3. Show original PAN card on camera  
4. Live photo + geo-location captured  
5. Digital signature via Aadhaar eSign  
6. Account activated instantly!

**Banks & apps using fastest Video KYC in 2025:**  
Jupiter, Fi Money, Niyo, IndusInd IndusMax, Groww, Zerodha, Angel One

## Central KYC Registry (CKYC) — Your One-Time KYC for Life

- One 14-digit CKYC number → valid across all banks, mutual funds, insurance  
- Do KYC once with any institution → never repeat  
- Check your CKYC status: https://www.ckycindia.in

**Pro Tip:** Always quote your CKYC number when opening new accounts — saves 5–10 minutes.

## Checklist Before Starting Paperless Onboarding

- [ ] Aadhaar linked with current mobile number  
- [ ] PAN card handy (physical or soft copy)  
- [ ] Good internet + front camera  
- [ ] Well-lit room (for Video KYC)  
- [ ] Blank white paper + black pen (to show live during V-CIP)  
- [ ] Updated address in Aadhaar (or self-declaration ready)
