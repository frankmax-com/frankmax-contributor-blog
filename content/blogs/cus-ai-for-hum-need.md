*In India, a good chatbot doesn't just answer — it speaks your language, knows your city, and pays via UPI in 5 seconds.*

## Introduction

A generic "Hi, how can I help you?" chatbot is dead in 2025.

Indian users want bots that:
- Speak Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu… fluently
- Understand "bhaiya loan chahiye" or "anna recharge pannu"
- Accept payments via UPI inside the chat
- Remember what they ordered last Diwali

This is **customised chatbot design** — and it's now table stakes for every business in India.

Here's exactly how the smartest brands are doing it.

## Step 1: Know Your Indian User Personas Deeply

| Persona                  | Language Preference | Peak Usage Time | Typical Queries                          |
|--------------------------|---------------------|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Tier-2/3 Housewife       | Hindi + local slang | 9 AM – 12 PM    | Recharge, bill payment, gold loan status |
| College Student          | Hinglish            | 10 PM – 2 AM    | Exam form, food delivery, EMI reminder   |
| Small Business Owner     | Regional language   | 7–10 AM         | GST filing, invoice, UPI collection      |
| Senior Citizen           | Hindi / Voice       | 8–11 AM         | Pension status, medicine delivery        |

Build separate flows for each — one bot, many personalities.

## Step 3: Add UPI Payments Inside the Chat (2025 Must-Have)

Best implementations:

1. **Axis Bank WhatsApp Banking**  
   → Type "pay 500 to Ravi" → UPI flow inside WhatsApp → done

2. **Myntra Bot**  
   → Browse → Add to cart → Pay via UPI → Track → All inside chat

3. **IRCTC Rail Mitra**  
   → Book ticket → Pay → Get PNR → Food order → All on WhatsApp

No app switching = 40% higher conversion

## Step 4: Personalisation That Actually Feels Personal

| Personalisation Level     | Example Message                                      | Conversion Impact |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Basic                     | Hi Kavinashri                                        | +5%               |
| Medium                    | Hi Kavinashri, your EMI of ₹8,450 is due in 3 days   | +18%              |
| Advanced                  | Namaste Kavinashri ji, aapka gold loan EMI kal hai. ₹2,000 pre-pay karein aur ₹180 interest bachayein? | +45%          |

Use first name + local greeting + past behaviour + pending action = magic

## Step 5: Voice + WhatsApp = The 2025 Winning Combo

- 68% Indian users prefer voice bots in regional languages
- Top voice bots: Gnani.ai, Skit.ai, Sarvam AI (Indian-accent trained)

**Real Example:**  
PhonePe's IVR → transferred to WhatsApp voice note → replied in Tamil voice → issue solved  
Zero hold time, 92% resolution rate

## Quick Checklist to Customise Your Chatbot in 2025

- [ ] Supports at least Hindi + 1 regional language  
- [ ] Trained on real Indian chat transcripts  
- [ ] UPI payment flow inside chat  
- [ ] Remembers user name, past orders, pending actions  
- [ ] Works flawlessly on WhatsApp (90% Indian users)  
- [ ] Has voice fallback for senior citizens  
- [ ] Switches tone based on user persona  
- [ ] A/B tests greetings weekly

## Conclusion

In 2025 India, a chatbot that speaks only English is like a shopkeeper who doesn't understand Hindi — it will lose customers.

The winners are building bots that feel like a helpful friend from your own city.

Start small:  
Add Hindi + your customer's name + one UPI flow.

Watch your engagement (and sales) explode.
