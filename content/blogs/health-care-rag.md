## The Bulletproof 2026 Architecture

1. Patient messages in any language (text or voice)

2. Retrieval from private, encrypted vector DB (updated every 5–15 min):
   - Hospital Management System (HMS) / HIS / LIS / RIS
   - Doctor OPD rosters & leave calendar
   - Package & test pricing (date-wise)
   - Official clinical guidelines & normal ranges
   - Past patient reports & prescriptions (with consent)

3. Retrieved facts + full conversation memory → LangGraph stateful agent

4. Agent decides: share report, book slot, collect symptoms, trigger payment, escalate to doctor, or send reminder

5. Response delivered via WhatsApp, website, or voice with 100% audit trail

→ **Zero hallucination. Full HIPAA/GDPR-style compliance. Complete memory across episodes.**
