## Introduction

Every second, Indian digital payment systems process thousands of transactions.  
Behind the scenes, powerful AI systems are watching every single one — looking for even the tiniest sign of fraud.

From ₹1 UPI transfers to ₹50 lakh NEFTs, nothing escapes the fraud detection net in 2025.

Here's exactly how banks and apps catch fraud before you even notice.

## Types of Fraud Banks Monitor 24×7 in India (2025)

| Fraud Type                  | Example                                      | Detected Using                     |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Account Takeover (ATO)      | Someone logs in using your stolen password   | Device + behaviour change          |
| Mule Account                | Fraudster uses your account to route money   | High-velocity inward + outward     |
| Phishing / Vishing          | Fake SMS asking OTP                          | Link analysis + known fraud DB     |
| First-Party Fraud           | Borrower lies about income                   | Document + alternative data check  |
| Card Not Present (CNP)      | Stolen card used online                      | 3DS + velocity checks              |
| UPI Fraud                   | Fake merchant QR, remote access scam         | Real-time behavioural scoring      |

## How Real-Time Transaction Monitoring Works (2025 Tech Stack)

1. **Device Fingerprinting**  
   → Phone model, OS version, screen resolution, fonts, time zone, IP geolocation  
   → Even if you clear cookies, banks know it's a new device

2. **Behavioural Analytics**  
   → Typing speed, mouse movement (web), swipe pattern (app)  
   → Suddenly transferring ₹5 lakh at 2 AM from a new city? Instant red flag

3. **Velocity Checks**  
   → 10 transactions in 5 minutes?  
   → ₹1 lakh received and sent out in 30 seconds? → Mule account alert

4. **AI/ML Risk Scoring**  
   → Every transaction gets a fraud score (0–999)  
   → Above 850 → auto-block + OTP + call verification

5. **Network & Graph Analytics**  
   → Connects the dots between phones, emails, bank accounts  
   → Detects fraud rings operating across cities

## RBI-Mandated Fraud Monitoring Systems (2025)

- Central Fraud Registry (CFR) — All banks report fraud within 24 hours
- Early Warning Signals (EWS) — Mandatory for loans above ₹5 crore
- Central Payment Fraud Information Registry (CPFIR) — Real-time UPI/IMPS fraud sharing
- Fraud Risk Management Directions 2024 — AI-based monitoring compulsory

## What Triggers an Instant Block in 2025?

| Trigger                                 | Typical Bank Action                     |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Transaction from new country            | Instant block + call                    |
| ₹50,000+ UPI from new device            | Extra OTP + biometric                   |
| Multiple failed OTP attempts            | Account freeze for 24 hours             |
| Money received from known fraud account | Incoming transaction reversed           |
| High-risk merchant payment              | 3D Secure + video KYC                   |

## Tips to Stay Safe (From Actual Fraud Analysts)
